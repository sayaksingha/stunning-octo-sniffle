Prosody data communications Protocols

Prosody defines a data communications protocol as a way of transporting data on a timeslot. Many of the supported protocols can transport data which is formatted in different ways, and in Prosody the packaging of data into these formats is called encoding. Encodings are described in Prosody Data Communications Encodings.

The protocols available are:

  • raw digital data transmitter and receiver
  • V.110
  • V.110 RLP transmitter and receiver
  • CW modem transmitter and receiver
  • FSK modem transmitter and receiver (e.g. V.21, V.23, etc)
  • V.27ter modem transmitter and receiver
  • V.29 modem transmitter and receiver
  • V.17 modem transmitter and receiver
  • V.32 modem transmitter and receiver
  • TTY receiver for ITU V.18 Annex A
  • CPU usage monitor
  • Heap usage monitor

The possible combinations of protocols and encodings are described in Prosody data communications Protocols and Encodings.
