pathtest tdmrx

Status codes

The tdmrx operations do not report status, so no status is shown.

Settings

The only setting used by tdmrx is the timeslot which is specified in the form: 

ts S:T:F

where S is the stream number, T is the timeslot number, and F is the data format. The formats available are:

aA law
umu law
ddata

This is a command understood by the pathtest test program.
